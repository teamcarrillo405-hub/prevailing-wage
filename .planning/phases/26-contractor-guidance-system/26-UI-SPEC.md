## New Components This Phase

| Component | Path | Props |
|-----------|------|-------|
| `HelpCallout` | `src/client/components/ui/HelpCallout.tsx` | `icon: LucideIcon`, `title: string`, `body: string`, `className?: string` |
| `TermTooltip` | `src/client/components/ui/TermTooltip.tsx` | `term: string`, `definition: string`, `className?: string` |

**HelpCallout visual pattern:** Wraps or mirrors `Card` token classes (`bg-surface-card rounded-card shadow-card border border-border-default`) with `padding="sm"` (p-4). Left border accent at 3px solid `--color-brand-gold`. Info icon (Lucide `Info`, 16px, `text-brand-gold`) left of title. Title at 14px/700, body at 14px/400. Always visible — no dismiss. Positioned below PageHeader, above page content.

**TermTooltip interaction contract:**
- Renders inline: `{term} <button aria-label="Definition of {term}"><QuestionIcon /></button>`
- QuestionIcon: Lucide `HelpCircle`, 14px, `text-text-secondary`, `hover:text-brand-gold`
- Tooltip panel: `position: absolute`, appears **above** the trigger (bias-up), falls back below when viewport collision detected
- Panel style: `bg-nav-dark text-white text-xs leading-relaxed p-3 rounded-sm max-w-xs shadow-card`
- Open/close: `useState` toggle. Opens on desktop hover (onMouseEnter/onMouseLeave) AND on click/tap (onClick toggle). Escape key closes. Click-outside closes via document event listener.
- No Radix, no Headless UI. Custom ~30 line implementation per D-01.
- Touch targets: `?` button minimum 24x24px (enforced via `p-1` + inline icon).

### TermTooltip Definitions

| Term | Definition (1-2 plain sentences) |
|------|----------------------------------|
| Davis-Bacon | A federal law requiring contractors on federal or federally funded construction projects to pay workers the locally prevailing wage for their trade. Wages are set by the Department of Labor and published on SAM.gov. |
| WH-347 | The Department of Labor's official certified payroll form. Contractors must submit it weekly to the contracting officer as proof that workers were paid the correct prevailing wage. |
| Prevailing wage | The minimum hourly pay rate for each trade and location, determined by the DOL based on what most workers in that area earn. Paying below this rate is a federal violation. |
| CWHSSA | The Contract Work Hours and Safety Standards Act. It requires overtime pay (1.5x base rate) for all hours over 40 in a week on federal contracts exceeding $100,000. |
| WD | Wage Determination — the specific DOL document that lists the prevailing wage rates for a given trade in a given county. Each project is assigned a WD number tied to its location. |
