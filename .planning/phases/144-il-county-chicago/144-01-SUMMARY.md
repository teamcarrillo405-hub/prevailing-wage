# Phase 144-01 Summary: Illinois County + Chicago Municipal System

## What Was Built

Infrastructure from Phase 135 supports IL county and Chicago municipal wage determinations. IL DOL publishes county rates for all 102 IL counties; Chicago CDOT/OEMC publishes municipal trade rates under Municipal Code § 2-92-330.

The `city` field on `county_wage_determinations` supports Chicago-specific rates distinct from Cook County state rates. IL county datalist included in ProjectForm.
